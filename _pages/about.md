My name is Soo Jeong Choi, and I am a researcher in the Department of AI Mobility Technology at the Korea Transport Institute (KOTI). I received my M.S. degree in Geography from Kyungpook National University, where I was affiliated with the [GIScience & Geospatial Bigdata Lab.](https://knu-giscience.github.io/) under [Prof. Jae Hyun Lee](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=_xIgyHUAAAAJ&hl=en).

My research interests lie in behavioral and transportation geography, focusing on mobility equity, and quality of life in the context of an aging society and climate resilience. I study person-based mobility and travel behavior using diverse geospatial datasets, including mobile phone location data, household travel surveys, and emerging forms of urban big data. Broadly, as transportation systems face rapid change from climate change, emerging mobility technologies, and shifting lifestyles such as remote work, I seek to understand how these forces reshape activity-travel patterns and shape equitable access to opportunities for underserved communities and aging populations. To do this, I am particularly interested in developing methodological approaches to extract meaningful behavioral patterns from data not originally designed for travel behavior research; for example, inferring trip purposes from geospatial information embedded in large-scale passive mobility data.

I am always happy to discuss research ideas and potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out to me at sjeongchoi21 at gmail.com !
